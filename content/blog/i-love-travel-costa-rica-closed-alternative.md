## What happened to I Love Travel Costa Rica?

As of August 2026, ILT posted a notice on their own website stating that **"due to unforeseen operational circumstances, I Love Travel Costa Rica has ceased operations and is no longer accepting new bookings."** Existing customers are being contacted by email and told to arrange alternative transportation.

The closure appears to have been sudden. If you had an upcoming transfer booked with them, the safest assumption is that **it will not operate** — don't wait at the curb hoping a van shows up.

## What to do if you had a reservation with ILT

Take these steps now, in order:

1. **Verify every upcoming transfer.** Check your email (including spam) for any cancellation notice from ILT. Assume unconfirmed transfers are not happening.
2. **Save all your documentation.** Booking confirmations, receipts, payment records, and any email or WhatsApp messages. You'll need these for a refund.
3. **Request a refund through your payment provider.** If you paid by credit card and the service won't be delivered, contact your card issuer about a **chargeback / dispute** for services not rendered. This is usually the most reliable path when a company has closed. PayPal and similar processors have comparable buyer-protection claims.
4. **Rebook your transportation as soon as possible.** Peak dates fill up fast, especially airport transfers and popular routes like La Fortuna and Manuel Antonio. The earlier you rebook, the more vehicle options you'll have.
